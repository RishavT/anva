## Limits and fix-forward policy

- Deploy the digest, not a mutable tag. Production operators still own unique
  secrets, TLS, exact trusted proxies, backups, and monitoring.
- Supported session re-entry, external object-store recovery, persistent
  observability, and managed-deployment controls remain post-MVP issues
  #37–#40. Canvas issue #49 preserves its unchanged 250 ms p95 target and its
  explicit post-MVP performance disposition.
- Decommission revokes access but is not verified legal erasure. Chromium is
  the browser acceptance target; other browsers remain unverified.

Source changes after `e89b06a`, including the finalizer correction on current
`main`, are not retroactively part of v0.1.6. This documentation reconciliation
is preparation for the next immutable fix-forward patch, `v0.1.7`. It must use a
new reviewed tag, decisions, approvals, artifacts, attestations, and installation
verification; no v0.1.6 approval may be replayed.

The v0.1.0 vulnerability exception is historical evidence only and does not
authorize v0.1.6 or a future v0.1.7 candidate.
