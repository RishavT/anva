# Provenance and claims

For every material organizational fact, requirement, policy, owner, decision,
or finding, cite normalized context-packet provenance containing:

- source URL;
- source locator;
- content hash;
- observation time.

An assertion, entity, policy, requirement, or work-item UUID is not provenance.
If normalized provenance is missing or no longer authorized, label the claim
unavailable and add a visible limitation. Do not silently promote it to fact.

Keep facts, requirements, assumptions, conflicts, suggestions, and limitations
in separate output fields or sections. Show freshness and inference labels.
Preserve current and stale sides of a conflict without choosing a winner.
